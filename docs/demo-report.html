The approval process requires Business Sponsor, Procurement, Information Security (mandatory), Finance, and Legal to review the intake request. Financial thresholds are determined by the annual commitment amount using the current matrix effective 2026-08-15: spend under USD 50k needs Budget owner/Procurement reviewer; USD 50k–$249,999 adds Department VP and Procurement Director; $250k–$999,999 adds CFO; $1M+ or regulated/high-risk vendors add COO (and require CFO/GC approval regardless of spend). Legal review is mandatory unless the renewal uses a standard template with no changes to data use, liability, term, governing law, security obligations, and risk is low below USD 100k.

- Approvals required: Business Sponsor → Procurement → Information Security (mandatory) → Finance → Legal
- Financial thresholds apply based on annual commitment amount per the matrix effective 2026-08-15
